It helps organizations understand performance and predict future outcomes.

2\. Organized the data in Excel.

\* Better understanding of business performance.

**Applications**



\* Sales forecasting

\* Market analysis

\* Business reporting

Trend analysis helps convert historical data into meaningful insights that support business decisions.
