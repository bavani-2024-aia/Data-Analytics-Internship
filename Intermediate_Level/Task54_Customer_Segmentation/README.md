**Task 54 - Customer Segmentation**



Customer segmentation is a method used to divide customers into different groups based on their characteristics, purchasing behavior, or interests. This helps businesses understand their customers better and provide products or services that match their needs.



For this task, a sample customer dataset was analyzed and grouped based on spending patterns. Customers with similar behavior were placed in the same category to make the data easier to understand.



Segmentation is commonly used in marketing campaigns, product recommendations, and customer relationship management. By identifying different customer groups, organizations can make better business decisions and improve customer satisfaction.



The analysis was performed using a simple dataset and visualized to observe how customers can be classified into meaningful groups.
